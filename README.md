### About

I design test strategies and build automation for web, API, and AI-powered applications. My work spans layered Playwright and Rest Assured frameworks, security and accessibility checks, and Claude skills that turn ticket-level QA into repeatable test runs and reports.

I'm a Technical Consultant in Quality Engineering at Perficient, where I own QA end to end on enterprise telecom and energy programs. Before that, I was the sole offshore QA on a conversational AI platform and later led a small QA team.
